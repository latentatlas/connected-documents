Try the complete review workflow

  1. Open Try the review and choose a work type.
  2. Read the confirmed source facts, calculations and content to preserve.
  3. Use the numbered document tabs to inspect the original text and recorded Nemotron proposal. Edit the right-hand box if needed.
  4. Inspect the field checks. They cover defined facts and anchors; they do not certify all meaning.
  5. Confirm that you reviewed the content, then select Accept my reviewed packet.
  6. Select Download my accepted packet. The ZIP contains your accepted document texts and a manifest distinguishing your edits from historical model output.
  7. Use Undo acceptance to restore the original pre-change texts. Restart this example loads the recorded proposal again.
